Overview

Mason syncs the tables you add from your Postgres and keeps them up to date. Tools query them at Mason's own Postgres URL.

Asking Mason…

Tables

The tables in your database. Add a table and Mason syncs it. Remove it and Mason stops syncing it and deletes its synced data.

Queries

Every query sent to Mason, newest first.

Users

Create and manage users that you can connect to tools and services.

Usage

What Mason answered in the time range: who connected, what ran, and the SQL it refused as unsupported. Also each synced table's size. Click a client or a card to see its queries on Queries.
